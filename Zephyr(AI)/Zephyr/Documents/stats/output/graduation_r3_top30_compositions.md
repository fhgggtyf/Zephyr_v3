## Tail diagnosis

- P95 is 806.47 DPS. There are 10,591 unique states at or above that cutoff,
  representing 5.014% of all ordered path weight.
- The top 30 unique states represent only 0.00547% of path weight. They cannot
  materially move the weighted P95 cutoff.
- Only 0.2847% of paths reach 900 DPS, 0.0395% reach 950 DPS, and 0.00118%
  reach 1000 DPS. The 1023.10 maximum is therefore a genuine extreme tail.
- The best composition spends its 12 upgrades on 2 attack, 2 attack-speed,
  3 crit-chance, 3 crit-damage and 2 flat-penetration stacks. This produces
  130 attack, 1.9 attacks/s, 85% crit at 2.7x, and 70 flat penetration.
- At this target, 70 flat penetration leaves only 2 effective defense after
  the initial 10% percentage penetration. Additional percentage penetration
  has very little room to contribute.
- Compared with the all-path average of 1.2 stacks in every attribute, P95+
  paths average 2.22 crit-chance, 1.77 attack, 1.72 flat-penetration, 1.57
  attack-speed and 1.47 crit-damage stacks. They average only 0.49 max-HP,
  0.32 percentage-penetration, and 0.81 stacks in each extra multiplier.

The maximum is driven by a specific multiplicative interaction between crit
chance, crit damage, attack, attack speed and enough flat penetration to nearly
remove defense. P95 itself is broader and is not an artifact of the top 30.
